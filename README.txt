========================================================================
                     VOIDKAGE — DESKTOP COMPANIONS
                     KAGE & KAGE-CHAN (Windows 10 / 11)
========================================================================

Thank you for downloading VOIDKAGE Desktop Companions!

This application gives you two living chibi companions directly on your
Windows desktop: KAGE (the mischievous shadow pet) and KAGE-CHAN (his cute
chibi anime companion).


QUICK START (NO PROGRAMMING OR SETUP REQUIRED):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If you downloaded the Portable ZIP:
   - Right-click "VOIDKAGE-Portable-x64.zip" -> Select "Extract All...".
   - Open the extracted folder.
   - Double-click "VOIDKAGE.exe".

2. If you downloaded the Installer:
   - Double-click "VOIDKAGE-Setup-1.0.0.exe".
   - Follow the setup wizard and launch.

3. On first launch:
   - The VOIDKAGE Welcome Screen will appear.
   - Choose which companions you want active (Kage, Kage-Chan, or both).
   - Click "Enter the Void".
   - Both characters will immediately appear on your desktop!


HOW TO INTERACT WITH YOUR COMPANIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- DRAG: Click and hold on a character, then drag anywhere on your screens.
- INTERACT: Click once on a character to open their radial interaction menu:
    [💬] Talk      - Chat with your companion
    [🐾] Pet       - Give them headpats and affection
    [⚡] Play      - Play a game or watch them do tricks
    [❓] Question  - Ask them a question
    [💤] Sleep     - Put them to rest
    [⚙️] Settings  - Open the full VOIDKAGE Dashboard
    [✖] Close     - Close the interaction menu
- SYSTEM TRAY: Look in the bottom-right Windows taskbar tray (by the clock)
  for the VOIDKAGE red-and-black icon. Right-click to change desktop modes,
  reset positions, toggle companions, or open settings.


DESKTOP OPERATING MODES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NORMAL MODE: Balanced chatter and cute idle animations.
- FOCUS MODE: Infrequent, gentle reminders to take breaks and hydrate.
- ACTIVE MODE: High energy, frequent jokes, banter, and playing together.
- QUIET MODE: Silent companions, zero popups or chatter.


PERMANENT WINDOWS STARTUP:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
VOIDKAGE can start automatically when your computer turns on and you log in.
To verify or change this:
1. Open VOIDKAGE Settings (double-click the tray icon).
2. Go to the "Startup Settings" tab.
3. Toggle "Launch on Windows Startup" to ON.
Your characters will quietly restore their positions and moods every time!


PRIVACY & SYSTEM FOOTPRINT:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 100% Offline: No internet connection is ever required.
- 100% Local: All memories and moods are stored in a local SQLite file
  in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\voidkage-companions\voidkage.sqlite).
- Zero background clutter: Clean single-instance execution.


TROUBLESHOOTING:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Characters got lost off-screen?
  Right-click the VOIDKAGE tray icon -> Select "Reset Desktop Positions".
- Windows SmartScreen notice?
  Because this is an open-source indie application, click "More info" ->
  "Run anyway".
- Need to exit?
  Right-click the VOIDKAGE tray icon -> Select "Exit VOIDKAGE".
