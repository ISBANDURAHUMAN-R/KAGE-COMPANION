KAGE Companion is an independently developed personal companion application
created by Isbandu Rahuman R. Official Ownership: Isbandu Rahuman R.

This application gives you two living chibi companions directly on your
Windows desktop: KAGE (the mischievous shadow pet) and KAGE-CHAN (his cute
chibi anime companion).


QUICK START (NO PROGRAMMING OR SETUP REQUIRED):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If you downloaded the Portable ZIP:
   - Right-click "KAGE-COMPANION-Portable-x64.zip" -> Select "Extract All...".
   - Open the extracted folder.
   - Double-click "KAGE COMPANION.exe".

2. If you downloaded the Installer:
   - Double-click "KAGE-COMPANION-Setup-1.0.0.exe".
   - Follow the setup wizard and launch.

3. On first launch:
   - The KAGE COMPANION Welcome Screen will appear.
   - Choose which companions you want active (Kage, Kage-Chan, or both).
   - Click "Start Companions".
   - Both characters will immediately appear on your desktop!


HOW TO INTERACT WITH YOUR COMPANIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- DRAG: Click and hold on a character, then drag anywhere on your screens.
- CLICK: Click once on a character to cycle through 15 official expressions
  and poses with sound chimes.
- RIGHT-CLICK: Right-click either character to open Companion Settings.
- RADIAL MENU: Click to open quick controls (Pet, Play, Sleep, Settings, Close).
- SYSTEM TRAY: Look in the bottom-right Windows taskbar tray (by the clock)
  for the KAGE COMPANION icon. Right-click to reset positions,
  toggle companions, or open settings.


DESKTOP COMPANION EXPERIENCE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SILENT & OBSERVANT: Your companions quietly float on your desktop without
  unsolicited speech popups or annoying interruptions.
- EXPRESSIVE: Click either companion to instantly cycle through authentic
  expressions and poses.
- SUBTLE IDLE SHIFTS: Occasional subtle shifts in posture keep them feeling
  alive without being distracting.


PERMANENT WINDOWS STARTUP:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
KAGE COMPANION can start automatically when your computer turns on and you log in.
To verify or change this:
1. Open KAGE COMPANION Settings (double-click the tray icon).
2. Go to the "Startup & Privacy" tab.
3. Toggle "Launch on Windows Startup" to ON.
Your characters will quietly restore their positions and moods every time!


PRIVACY & SYSTEM FOOTPRINT:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 100% Offline: No internet connection is ever required.
- 100% Local: All memories and moods are stored in a local SQLite file
  in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\kage-companion\kage.sqlite).
- Zero background clutter: Clean single-instance execution.


TROUBLESHOOTING:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Characters got lost off-screen?
  Right-click the KAGE COMPANION tray icon -> Select "Reset Desktop Positions".
- Windows SmartScreen notice?
  Because this is an open-source indie application, click "More info" ->
  "Run anyway".
- Need to exit?
  Right-click the KAGE COMPANION tray icon -> Select "Exit KAGE COMPANION".
