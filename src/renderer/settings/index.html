Privacy & Local Storage

KAGE COMPANION is 100% offline. All companion coordinates, scales, and preferences are stored locally in your Windows user profile.

Database Engine: SQLite 3 (Local Embedded WebAssembly)
Storage Location: %APPDATA%\kage-companion\kage.sqlite
Network Requests: Zero (100% Offline)

About the Companions

KAGE Desktop Companions live on your Windows desktop above your windows. Featuring KAGE the mischievous shadow pet and KAGE-CHAN the chibi girl, they react smoothly to mouse interactions, stay in place, and quietly keep you company as you browse, write code, or relax.
