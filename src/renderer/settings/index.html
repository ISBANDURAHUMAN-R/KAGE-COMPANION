Design Philosophy

VOIDKAGE companions live on your Windows desktop above your windows. Featuring KAGE the mischievous shadow pet and KAGE-CHAN the caring anime chibi girl, they react, banter, and keep you company as you write code, browse, or game.

Platform: Windows 10 / 11 64-bit
Colors: Black × Blood Red × White
Engine: Electron + SQLite3 + Web Audio
